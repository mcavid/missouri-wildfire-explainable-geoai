# Data, model and figure attribution

The MIT license applies to project-owned source code and accompanying software
documentation. It does not replace third-party dataset terms or automatically
license input data, derived datasets, trained models, or rendered scientific figures.

The repository supplies derived modeling tables, modeled grid geometry, saved
predictions, estimators and figure inputs for research reproducibility. Cite Javid
and Amer and the relevant source providers when using these materials. Provider
license/attribution terms continue to govern their contributions. The authors have
not selected a separate blanket license for derived data, models or figure assets;
request permission for reuse beyond rights granted by the underlying sources.

Source families include USGS 3DEP/NLCD and LANDFIRE, USFS/GTAC canopy, NASA MODIS,
PRISM, gridMET, WorldPop, Census TIGER/Line, NIFC InFORM and Missouri boundary data.
WorldPop publishes population products under CC BY 4.0; US government datasets
and MODIS products have their stated provider terms. Consult the exact provider
documentation listed in docs/data_sources.md. Raw acquisition files are not bundled.
